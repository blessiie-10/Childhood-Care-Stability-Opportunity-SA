# Dashboard and Presentation Guide

## 

## Interactive dashboard

Open the private dashboard: [Childhood Care, Stability and Opportunity in South Africa](https://childhood-care-sa.singo-blessing10.chatgpt.site)

The single-page dashboard is organised as a guided data story:

1. National living-arrangement distribution and validation benchmark.
2. Interactive attendance and grade-repetition comparison with observed confidence intervals and adjusted point estimates.
3. A living-arrangement selector that updates household income, food insufficiency, fixed internet access, rural location and grant receipt.
4. A concise methodology and limitations section.

The dashboard is keyboard-accessible, responsive for smaller screens and retains responsible interpretation language alongside the findings.

## Portfolio presentation

The eight-slide presentation follows this narrative:

1. Research question and represented population.
2. National co-residence distribution.
3. Unequal household resources.
4. Adjusted school-attendance results.
5. Adjusted grade-repetition results.
6. Social-protection reach.
7. Reproducible analytical method and limitations.
8. Implications focused on support conditions rather than ranking family types.

All visible estimates come from the reproducible output tables in `outputs/tables/`. Speaker notes provide short presentation guidance without crowding the slides.

## Uncertainty convention

Observed education estimates include design-aware 95% confidence intervals using the survey strata, primary sampling units and person weights. Adjusted education values remain standardised point estimates and are labelled accordingly in both deliverables.
